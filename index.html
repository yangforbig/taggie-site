Not another public feed. Taggie brings reading and personal reflection together in a private library you can return to.

01 / Bring it in

From saved to read.

Import supported EPUB and Markdown files, or convert a supported X article into EPUB and Markdown. Source availability and content rights still apply.

02 / Make a mark

A thought, not just a highlight.

Keep passages, write notes, and reflect before marking a reading complete. Your words sit alongside what prompted them.

03 / Come back

A shelf of your own.

Read saved files offline. Optionally sign in for a private Supabase archive. File restore is available; complete cross-device note and reflection sync is not.

Small by intention. Still in progress.

An honest
private beta.

Taggie is an independently developed personal reading app, not a public content catalogue. There is no public App Store download or open registration offered on this website.

Cloud onboarding and recovery are still being validated. X sign-in is planned and currently disabled; converting an article is a separate feature, not proof of an X login.

  • Local reading works without an account.
  • Imported files are not published for other readers.
  • Only deliberately shared comments reach eligible matching-edition readers.
